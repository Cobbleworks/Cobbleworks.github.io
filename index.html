NPCs · Flagship

Blockfolk

NPCs that look like players and act like residents. Give them a daily routine, a shop, a quest, a fight, or a personality, all from in-game menus.

  • No YAML editing. Skins, gear, dialog, routes, and behaviour are set up in menus.
  • They do things. Walk routes, mine, harvest crops, move items between chests, and run shops.
  • They fight. Custom abilities, alliances, loot, respawn timers, and boss bars.
  • Optional AI. Connect OpenRouter and they chat in character. They can only take actions you have allowed.
  • Quests. Spawned NPCs appear in BeautyQuests' NPC selector.

Redstone

Wireless Redstone

Link lamps, copper bulbs, and containers into named groups. Toggle one and the whole group follows, however far apart they are.

  • Lamps and bulbs switch together across any distance, including every copper and waxed variant.
  • Shared containers. Chests, barrels, copper chests, and all 17 shulker colours can share one inventory in real time.
  • Hopper friendly. Feed one end of a group and sort from the other.
  • Circuit tool to add blocks and inspect groups with colour-coded outlines.
  • No dependencies. Drop in the JAR and start linking.

Events

Blood Moon

Some nights, the moon turns red. Seven hand-built boss encounters stalk your players until sunrise, then disappear without a trace.

  1. VampireStarts disguised. Drains health, summons bats, and gets nastier near defeat.
  2. WitchCycles spell phases: curses, cages, mirror clones, and life drain.
  3. WerewolfLeaps, makes targets bleed, calls the pack, and turns feral.
  4. GhostHard to hit. Bring a light source to reveal it.
  5. ScarecrowFear, drains, and area denial in stronger harvest phases.
  6. ClownFireworks, decoys, teleport tricks, and a manic final phase.
  7. ZombieInfection, toxic ground, a horde, then a berserk finish.
  • Four difficulty profiles, from easy to nightmare, switchable without a restart.
  • Your rules. Choose worlds, odds, rewards, and population limits.
  • Test any boss on demand with /bloodmoon spawn witch.

Powered by Blockfolk. The bosses are Blockfolk NPCs, so install both.

  • MC 26.2
  • Java 25+
  • Needs Blockfolk 1.4.0+

Music

Custom Jukebox

Bring your own Note Block Studio songs. Players browse a folder-organised library, admins set up music signs, and any song can be pressed onto a record that plays in a normal jukebox.

  • Personal player with queue, shuffle, loop, and volume
  • Jukebox signs with toggle, pulse, or ignore redstone modes
  • Custom records survive restarts and work with hoppers

/jukebox play

  • Paper
  • MC 26.2
  • Java 25+
  • Needs NoteBlockAPI 1.7.0+

Maps

Map Revealer

Hold a map, run one command, and it is filled in, without walking every chunk. Or build a wall of item frames and let it keep itself up to date as the world changes.

  • Every map scale, plus Nether and End rendering
  • Nine themed palettes and fixed Y-level slices for cave maps
  • Self-refreshing map walls with player markers and labels

/revealmap sepia

  • MC 1.20.4
